## Format conventions

- Greeting: "Hi <name>," for cold/warm; drop the greeting on internal thread replies.
- Sign-off: "Thanks," for cold/formal; "—" dash + first name for warm/internal.
- Length: under 150 words for a first contact; shorter for replies.
- One ask per email. Put the CTA in its own line near the end.
