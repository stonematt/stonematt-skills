# <Project> product-lead lessons

One dated section per session with something to learn. Unfolded lessons load at every product-lead open.

Each lesson carries a scope. A `[project]` lesson folds into this repo's product brief or a persona file; mark it `folded <sha>` once that commit lands. A `[skill]` lesson is about a skill itself (product-lead, release-manager, or another): report it to `~/.claude/skill-workbench/<skill>/feedback.md` and mark it `reported <skill> workbench <date>`; the fold is marked in that ledger.
